No new risks were introduced in the window (nothing changed). The standing
risks from the 2026-07-31 report remain, with current status:

- **Exactly-once boundary (unchanged).** A job row stored before the provider
  accepts can still lose a paid generation to an interruption. PR #15 (the
  2026-08-01 gap commit) improved *read-path* resilience and added no-paid
  resume for exhausted tracking, but the create-path gap itself is unaddressed.

  in-process poller safer (bounded transient-failure budget, recoverable

  polling, or webhooks.

  `http(s)` only; no allowlist or server-side media ingestion yet.
