## Risks

No new risks this window — no application, dependency, or deployment files
changed. The following carried-forward risks from earlier reports remain
accurate at branch head `79b87ec` (re-verified, not assumed):

- **Exactly-once boundary (pre-existing, documented)** — a job row is stored
  before the provider call; interruption after provider acceptance but before
  task-id persistence can orphan a paid generation. Startup recovery mitigates,
  not eliminates, this.
- **Prompt-override surface (`389ce61`)** — a client-authored `prompt`
  override becomes the generated text; correctness rests on the server-side
  guard that always renders/validates the immutable version with `values`
  first. Shipped and tested, but still the newest risky seam in
  `generationService.ts`.
- **Single-instance polling** — in-process poller only; no durable queue,
  multi-instance polling, or webhooks.

  allowlist or server-side media ingestion yet.
